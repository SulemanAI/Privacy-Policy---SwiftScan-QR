Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed by SwiftScan QR ("we," "our," or "us") when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect and Use

Our primary goal is to provide a fast and secure QR scanning experience. The App requires specific permissions and uses third-party services to function correctly:

  • Camera Access: The App requires permission to access your device's camera strictly for the purpose of scanning QR codes and barcodes in real-time. We do not record, store, or transmit your camera feed or images to any external servers. All scanning is processed locally on your device using Google ML Kit.
  • Photo Library/Gallery Access: The App provides a feature to scan QR codes from images saved on your device. If you use this feature, the App will request access to your photo library. The selected images are processed entirely locally on your device to extract the QR code data. We do not upload, save, or share your photos.
  • Advertising Data: The App uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect and use advertising data, such as your Android Advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data, to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

2. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Please review the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the App:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Google ML Kit

3. Data Retention and Security

Because the App processes QR codes locally and does not require user registration, we do not store your personal data on any servers. Any scanned data or history is kept entirely local to your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us at our developer email provided on the Google Play Store listing.
